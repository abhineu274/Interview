## Structure Breakdown (why it's memorable)

1. **Who I am** — software engineer first (React/.NET/Azure), GenAI as an extension, not the whole identity.
2. **Experience arc** — 4+ years engineering overall, 2+ years layering in GenAI/Azure (shows depth before specialization).
3. **Foundation** — healthcare staffing platform: full-stack ownership (frontend, backend, DB) — proves core engineering chops.
4. **Proof point** — DIP project: pipeline design + Service Bus migration (backend/distributed-systems strength) *plus* GenAI document understanding — the clearest example of "both."
5. **Current work** — GreenShield at IBM (shows the same balance is active today).
6. **Closing line** — explicitly states the positioning: "software engineer first, with strong GenAI expertise."

## Memorization Tips
- Practice it out loud 5–10 times until it flows without reading.
- Don't memorize word-for-word — memorize the **6 beats above** and speak naturally around them.
- Keep pace calm; pause briefly after "...combining solid backend/distributed-systems design with GenAI capabilities" — this is the line that signals balance, let it land.
- If the interviewer's role is more software-engineering-heavy, lean slightly more on beats 2–3; if it's more GenAI-heavy, lean slightly more on beats 3–4. The beats stay the same either way.
